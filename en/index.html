01 / 08Office suite1.3.4 · Released

Reality Office

Documents, spreadsheets and presentations. Open, edit and save Office files on the headset.

Write in Pages, analyse data in Sheets and build decks in Slides. All three editors work offline, and you can open a second file in its own window to work side by side.

Download APKSource codeWebsite

What it does

  • Pages

    Heading styles and outline navigation, a table of contents with page numbers, pictures, tables, footnotes, headers and footers. Track changes, comments and Compare.

  • Sheets

    Formulas across worksheets such as SUMIFS and XLOOKUP, Goal Seek, frozen panes, multi-column sort and filter, drop-down lists, conditional formatting, and column, line and pie charts.

  • Slides

    Layouts, alignment guides, tables and charts, transitions and animations, notes and rehearsal timings. A presenter console, and Present on Wall places the show on a wall the headset detects.

  • Side-by-side windows

    Open another file in a new window to draft against a brief or check a workbook while you update a chart.

  • Common formats

    Open and save DOCX, ODT, XLSX, ODS, PPTX, ODP and CSV, and open DOC, RTF, XLS and PPT. Every editor exports PDF.

  • AutoSave and recovery

    No internet permission. AutoSave keeps edits on disk and AutoRecover restores a draft after an interruption. Content it cannot edit, such as macros, prompts you to save a copy first.

02 / 08Image editor1.0.0

Reality Image Studio

A layer-based image editor with Photoshop PSD import and export.

The canvas floats in its own window with tools on the left and panels on the right. Layers, masks, adjustment layers, filters and retouching all run on the headset.

What it does

  • Layers

    Pixel, type, shape and adjustment layers; groups, layer masks and clipping masks; 27 blend modes; eight layer styles including Bevel & Emboss, Stroke and Drop Shadow.

  • PSD in and out

    Import and export 8-bit RGB and grayscale PSD with layers, groups, blend modes, masks and guides. The native .ris project keeps everything editable.

  • Adjustments and filters

    16 non-destructive adjustments such as Levels, Curves and Hue/Saturation, and 40 filters including Camera Raw, Liquify, Tilt-Shift, Smart Sharpen and Reduce Noise.

  • Retouch and select

    Spot Healing, Clone Stamp and Content-Aware Fill; Quick Selection, Magic Wand and Color Range, with expand, contract and feather.

  • 32 tools

    Brushes, gradients, dodge and burn, type and shapes, crop and straighten, Free Transform with perspective, and a full History panel.

  • Spatial Layer View

    Spread the whole layer stack out in depth in your room and inspect it layer by layer.

03 / 08Video editor1.0.1 · Released

Reality Video Editor

Offline video editing, from rough cut through color and sound to export.

Your footage stays on the device, with no account or internet connection. In the ultrawide layout the source and timeline monitors sit side by side above the tracks.

ReleasesSource code

What it does

  • Organise footage

    A media pool with scene, shot and take logging, favorite flags, keywords and smart bins. Generate proxies for large camera files.

  • Edit

    In and out points, insert and overwrite, Match Frame; ripple and slip trims and J/L cuts; up to eight video and eight audio tracks, with keyboard shortcuts.

  • Multicam and keyframes

    Sync a recorder to camera audio by waveform and switch angles while the dialogue continues. Scale keyframes and speed ramps.

  • Color

    Primary wheels, stills and split view, waveform and RGB parade, .cube LUTs, tracked windows and several grade versions per clip.

  • Sound

    Record a voiceover, EQ and de-esser, music ducking under speech and mix buses, with LUFS loudness and true-peak meters.

  • Deliver

    Export H.264 or H.265 video, stills or WAV; 9:16 and square frames; SRT and WebVTT captions; hand off FCPXML 1.10 or CMX 3600 EDL timelines.

04 / 08Industrial design reviewDesign preview

Reality CAD

A spatial CAD workbench for reviewing industrial design.

This is a design preview. You can try the whole interface and workflow with the built-in sample models; opening and exporting real CAD files is not connected yet.

What it does

  • Feature tree and assemblies

    Sketches, extrudes, lofts, fillets, shells, drafts and patterns are listed in order with editable parameters; assemblies show their parts and mates.

  • Viewport

    A view cube and dimensions, with zebra, curvature and section views for checking surfaces.

  • Space View

    Take the model out of the window and look at it in your room at 1:1 scale.

  • Drawings

    A 2D DXF drawing view with views, dimensions and a title block.

  • Deliver

    An export queue, delivery records, batch conversion and a reference table of 52 CAD formats.

  • Design preview

    Uses built-in sample data to show the whole flow, so you can evaluate the interface and interaction.

05 / 08NAS video player1.2.1 · Released

Reality NAS Player

Connect your NAS and media servers, browse films by poster and watch them in a VR cinema.

It reads titles, years, season and episode numbers and existing NFO files, and arranges films, series, documentaries and anime into a poster wall you can filter, pick episodes from and resume.

ReleasesSource codeWebsite

What it does

  • Sources

    SMB, WebDAV, NFS, FTP, SFTP and DLNA shares, plus Jellyfin, Emby and Plex servers, or video files on the headset.

  • Automatic matching

    Posters and summaries come from public film catalogues, with no sign-up or key for the first scan. A personal TMDB key is optional.

  • Poster wall

    Filter by type, open a series to choose a season and episode, pick between versions of a film and resume where you stopped.

  • Formats

    MKV, MP4, MOV, WebM, TS/M2TS, AVI and other common files, and compatible unencrypted Blu-ray and DVD backups (ISO, BDMV, VIDEO_TS).

  • Subtitles and audio

    SRT, WebVTT, ASS/SSA, PGS and more; switch embedded or external subtitles and audio tracks.

  • Cinemas and 360°

    Choose one of four VR cinemas, or watch VR180 and 360° video. The macOS and Windows apps share the same library engine.

06 / 08File transfer1.0.0

Reality File Transfer

Send files between PICO, Mac and Windows over your local network, without the cloud.

Devices on the same network find each other. Send in both directions between any number of PICO headsets, Macs and PCs, with no account.

What it does

  • Automatic discovery

    Devices on the same Wi-Fi appear automatically. If one doesn’t, add it by the IP address it shows.

  • Many devices, both ways

    Send to several devices at once, in any direction between PICO, Mac and PC.

  • Folders and resume

    Send whole folders with their structure. Transfers resume after a dropped connection, and a failed one can be continued.

  • USB first

    A PICO connected by cable is labelled USB and listed first.

  • Headset file browser

    On PICO, browse the headset’s pictures, videos, audio, documents, APKs and archives with thumbnails.

  • Confirm incoming

    Incoming files ask for confirmation, and you can choose to accept a device automatically from then on.

07 / 08Archive manager1.0 release candidate

Reality Unzip

Open, preview, extract and create archives on the headset.

Built on 7-Zip, Zstandard, LZ4 and Brotli. Look inside an archive’s text and pictures without extracting, then extract only what you need.

What it does

  • Common formats

    ZIP, 7z, RAR, TAR, GZ, BZ2, XZ, ZST, LZ4, Brotli, ISO, CAB, WIM, DMG and more, including split volumes.

  • Preview without extracting

    Read text files and view pictures inside the archive. Search text, jump to a line and change the encoding.

  • Extract what you need

    Extract single files or folders, work in batches, and choose what happens with files of the same name.

  • Create archives

    Pack files into an archive, with an optional password.

  • Verify and compare

    Test an archive’s integrity, compute and compare SHA-256 hashes, and compare two files byte by byte.

  • Folder usage

    See how much space a folder takes and list its files, then export the list.

08 / 08Proxy client1.0.0

Reality Rocket

A network proxy client built on the Mihomo core.

It bundles the Mihomo (Clash.Meta) core for arm64. Profiles, nodes, rules, connections and logs each have their own page on the headset.

What it does

  • Import profiles

    Clash YAML, Base64 subscriptions, and vless, vmess, trojan, ss, hysteria2, tuic, socks5, http and wireguard links, with scheduled subscription updates.

  • Push from your phone

    Scan a QR code shown in the headset and send a subscription or profile from your phone’s browser, instead of typing a long link.

  • Routing

    Rule, global and direct modes; custom rules and a rule-match tester; per-app routing, with local network traffic sent directly.

  • Status and logs

    Live traffic graph, current connections and the core’s log.

  • Connectivity check

    Test latency to Google, Cloudflare, GitHub and other common sites in one go.

  • Data and languages

    Bundled GeoIP and GeoSite data that you can update in Settings; 26 interface languages, including right-to-left.
